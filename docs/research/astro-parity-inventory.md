### Live hosting facts

- `www.eyuan.me/*` returns 301 to `eyuan.me/*` (the apex is primary). `/about` returns 301 to `/about/`.
- Unknown paths return 404 with the body of `dist/404.html`.
- HTML, xml and txt are served with `public,max-age=0,must-revalidate`. Hashed assets and public files get `public, max-age=14400, must-revalidate` (no immutable), plus ETag and br. HSTS is 1 year.
- The site is Netlify behind Cloudflare. The repo has no `_headers` or `_redirects`.
- Builds are deterministic: Node 22 (CI) and Node 26 (local) produce the same hashes as the live site.

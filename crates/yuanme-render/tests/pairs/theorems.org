#+NAME: thm:fermat
#+begin_theorem Fermat's little theorem
If \(p\) is prime, then \(a^p \equiv a \pmod p\).
#+end_theorem

#+BEGIN_PROOF
By induction on \(a\):

\[
(a + 1)^p \equiv a^p + 1
\]
#+END_PROOF

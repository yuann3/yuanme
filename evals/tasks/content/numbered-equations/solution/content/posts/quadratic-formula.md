---
title: Deriving the quadratic formula
date: 2026-03-01
description: Completing the square, one numbered step at a time.
tags: [algebra]
---

Start from a quadratic with $a \ne 0$:

$$
\begin{align}
ax^2 + bx + c &= 0 \label{eq:start} \\
x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x &= -\frac{c}{a} \label{eq:divide} \\
\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 &= \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2} \label{eq:square} \\
x &= \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \label{eq:formula}
\end{align}
$$

Each step keeps the roots of [](#eq:start), so [](#eq:formula) gives every root of it.

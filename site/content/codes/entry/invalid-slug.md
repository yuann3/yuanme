---
title: "entry/invalid-slug"
description: "A `slug:` override isn't a valid Slug."
---

A `slug:` override isn't a valid Slug. A Slug is lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_`, in segments separated by `/`, and an override is used exactly as written.

## Example

```
---
title: Pew
slug: My Post
---
```

## Fix

Use the corrected form the help suggests, here `slug: my-post`.
